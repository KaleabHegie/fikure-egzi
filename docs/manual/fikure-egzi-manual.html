  2. ሚናውን ይምረጡ። ከፅህፈት ቤት አስተዳዳሪ በስተቀር ለሁሉም ሚናዎች ሰውየው የሚሠራበትን የአገልግሎት ክፍል ወይም ክፍሎች ይምረጡ። ከሥራቸው ያሉትን ንዑስ ክፍሎችም በራሱ ያገኛሉ።
  3. ለሰውየው ኢሜይሉንና የመጀመሪያ የይለፍ ቃሉን ይስጡት፤ እንዲቀይረውም ይንገሩት።

በቦት ራስን መመዝገብን ማብራትና ማጥፋት

ወደ ማስተካከያዎች ይሂዱና በራስን መመዝገብ ስር አዲስ ሰዎች ራሳቸውን እንዲመዘግቡ ፍቀድን ያብሩ። ይህ በርቶ እያለ ስልክ ቁጥሩ በአባልነት መዝገብ ላይ የሌለ ማንኛውም ሰው በቴሌግራም ቦቱ ራሱን መመዝገብ ይችላል። የምዝገባ ዘመቻው ሲያልቅ ያጥፉት፤ ለውጡ በጥቂት ሰከንዶች ውስጥ ይሠራል።

ሌሎች ሥራዎች

ፅህፈት ቤቱ ከታች ለአገልግሎት ክፍል አስተዳዳሪዎች የተገለጸውን ሁሉ (አቴንዳንስ፣ ኮርሶች፣ ፈተናዎች፣ ንብረት) ለሁሉም ክፍሎች መሥራት ይችላል። ኦዲት ማን ምን መቼ እንደቀየረ ያሳያል።

የሚና መመሪያ

የአገልግሎት ክፍል አስተዳዳሪDepartment administrator

የአገልግሎት ክፍል አስተዳዳሪ የራሱን ክፍል ወይም ክፍሎች፣ ከሥራቸው ያሉትን ንዑስ ክፍሎች ጨምሮ ይመራል። የክፍሎቹን አባላት ያያል፤ ነገር ግን የአባላትን መረጃ መመዝገብም ማስተካከልም አይችልም። ለዚያ ፅህፈት ቤቱን ይጠይቁ።

የራስ ክፍሎችና ንዑስ ክፍሎችየአባላትን መረጃ ማስተካከል አይችልም

የክፍልዎ ሰዎች

  • የአገልግሎት ክፍሎችን ከፍተው ክፍልዎን ይምረጡ፤ አባላትን ይጨምሩና ሚናቸውን ይወስኑ።
  • ከአባላትዎ ማንኛውንም ከፍተው መረጃውን፣ አቴንዳንሱን፣ ኮርሶቹንና ፈተናዎቹን ይመልከቱ።
  • የአገልግሎት ምድቦችን ያደራጁ፤ ክፍልዎ የሚመራቸውን ክፍሎች ያስተዳድሩ።

አቴንዳንስ

  1. ወደ አቴንዳንስ ይሂዱና አዲስ ክፍለ ጊዜ ይጫኑ።
  2. ለምን እንደሆነ ይምረጡ፦ የአንድ ክፍል ደረጃ፣ የአገልግሎት ምድብ፣ ወይም እንደ ስብሰባ ያለ ሌላ መሰባሰብ። የስም ዝርዝሩ ከምርጫው በራሱ ይሞላል።
  3. ቀኑንና የመጀመሪያ ሰዓቱን ያስገቡ። ከመጀመሪያው ሰዓት ከ15 ደቂቃ በኋላ የሚገቡ በራሱ «አርፍዷል» ይባላሉ፤ ደቂቃዎቹን መቀየር ይችላሉ።
  4. QR ኮዶችን በመቃኘት ወይም የእያንዳንዱን ሰው ሁኔታ በመጫን አቴንዳንስ ይውሰዱ። ሲያልቅ ክፍለ ጊዜውን ዝጋ ይጫኑ። ስሕተት ለማረም በኋላ እንደገና ክፈት ይጫኑ።

ከአቴንዳንስ ያዦች በተለየ የአገልግሎት ክፍል አስተዳዳሪዎች ላለፉ ወይም ለሚመጡ ቀናት ክፍለ ጊዜዎችን መፍጠርና መቀየር ይችላሉ። አቴንዳንስ → ሪፖርቶች በብዛት የሚመጡትን እና ከተወሰነ መጠን በታች የወረዱትን ያሳያል፤ በዚህም ማንን መከታተል እንዳለብዎ ያውቃሉ።

ኮርሶች

በኮርሶች ስር ኮርስ ይመዝግቡ፣ ዙር (ቀን የተወሰነለት የኮርሱ አንድ ዙር) ያዘጋጁ፣ ተማሪዎችን ይጨምሩና መምህራንን ይመድቡ። ከዚያ መምህራኑ ምዘናዎቹን አዘጋጅተው ውጤት ያስገባሉ፤ እርስዎም ይህን ማድረግ ይችላሉ።

ፈተናዎች

ፈተናዎች የኮርስ አካል ያልሆኑ የአገልግሎት ክፍል መመዘኛዎች ናቸው፤ ለምሳሌ የመዝሙር ክፍል የቅዱስ ዮሐንስ ወረብ መመዘኛ። እያንዳንዱ ፈተና የአንድ ክፍል ነው፤ ከፈለጉ የአንድ ደረጃ ብቻ ሊሆን ይችላል።

  1. ወደ ፈተናዎች ይሂዱ፣ አዲስ ፈተና ይጫኑ፣ ክፍሉን ይምረጡ፤ ለአንድ ደረጃ ብቻ ከሆነ ደረጃውንም ይምረጡ።
  2. መመዘኛዎቹንና ድርሻቸውን ያስገቡ፤ ለምሳሌ እንቅስቃሴ 40% እና ዜማ 60%። የድርሻዎቹ ድምር 100 መሆን አለበት። ካልቀየሩት በስተቀር ማለፊያ ነጥቡ 50 ነው።
  3. ተፈታኞችን ይጨምሩ፦ አንድን ደረጃ ወይም ክፍሉን በሙሉ በአንድ ጊዜ፣ ወይም አባላትን በስማቸው ወይም በመለያ ቁጥራቸው ፈልገው አንድ በአንድ። በኋላ ክፍሉን የሚቀላቀሉም በዚሁ መንገድ ይጨመራሉ።
  4. የእያንዳንዱን ሰው ውጤት በውጤት ሰንጠረዡ ላይ ያስገቡ። እያንዳንዱ መመዘኛ ከድርሻው ይታረማል፤ ስለዚህ ዜማ ከ60 ነው። ሁሉም መመዘኛዎች ሲታረሙ ድምሩና ማለፍ ወይም መውደቁ ይታያል።

ንብረት

በንብረት ስር ክፍልዎ ያለውን ንብረት ከፎቶ ጋር ይመዝግቡ፤ በዚህም በቀላሉ ይገኛል፣ ይቆጠራልም።

የእርስዎ አቴንዳንስ ያዦችና መምህራን

All staff

Signing inወደ ሲስተሙ መግባት

Staff accounts are created by the office, or by a department administrator for their own attendance takers and teachers. Nobody can create their own staff account.

  1. Open portal.fikureegzi4.com in a browser on a phone or computer.
  2. Type the email and password you were given, then press ግባ (Sign in).
  3. On your first sign-in, open your name in the top corner, choose My profile (የእኔ መገለጫ) and change the password to one only you know.

Things every user can do

  • Change the language. The language switch in the top bar flips every screen between Amharic and English.
  • Change light or dark look. The theme button sits beside the language switch.
  • See only what your role allows. The menu on the left only shows the pages you can use. If a page you need is missing, ask the office to check your role.
  • Read dates in the Ethiopian calendar. Every date in the system and the bot is shown and typed in the Ethiopian calendar (ዓ.ም).
Keep your password to yourself.Every change in the system is recorded with the name of the person who made it. Sign out with your name → Sign out on shared computers.

Role guide

Office administratorየፅህፈት ቤት አስተዳዳሪ

The office sees and manages everything, across every department. It is the only role that registers and edits member records, changes office-wide settings, and creates department administrators.

All departmentsAll pages

Register a new member

  1. Go to Members (አባላት) and press Register member.
  2. Fill the sections: personal details, contact and address, education and work, emergency contact, and health. Only the first name, father's name, gender, date of birth and joining date are required. Add the phone number too, because the member needs it to use the bot and the member page.
  3. Under Classes (ክፍሎች), choose the level the member is in for each class they attend.
  4. If a chosen class has an agreement (for example the Mezmur Class), an agreement switch appears. Turn it on only after the member has read and accepted the rules.
  5. Save. The member gets a member ID such as FE-00042, which is also their QR code.
Many members at onceThe members list also has an Excel import. Download the template, fill it, upload it, fix any rows the check flags, then create them all in one go.

Keep member records up to date

  • Open a member from the list to see everything about them: details, classes, departments, attendance, courses and exams. Press Edit to change their record or photo.
  • Use the filters on the members list to narrow by class, department, status or how they registered. Choose Registered via → Self-registered (bot) to review people who registered themselves.
  • Mark someone Inactive instead of deleting them when they stop coming, so their history is kept.

Print QR membership cards

On the members list, press Print QR codes to print A4 sheets of QR codes, 20 per page, with the name and member ID under each. It prints every member that matches the list's current filters, so filter first. On a member's own page, Print QR prints just theirs.

Classes and their agreements

Under Classes (ክፍሎች), each class (for example the Mezmur Class) has levels. To give a class rules that members must accept, open the class menu and choose Add agreement, then write one rule per line. The agreement then appears both in the member form and at the end of bot registration for anyone who picks that class.

Departments

  • Departments (የአገልግሎት ክፍሎች) shows the nine main departments and their sub-departments. Switch between Cards and Tree to see the whole structure at once; the tree has a search box.
  • Open a department to see its roster. Add members to it and set their role: Head (ሰብሳቢ), Deputy (ምክትል), Secretary (ጸሐፊ) or Member.
  • Service groups organises members into groups per service term.

Staff accounts

  1. Go to Users (ተጠቃሚዎች) and press New user.
  2. Choose the role. For every role except Office administrator, also choose the department or departments the person works in. They automatically get the sub-departments under them too.
  3. Give the person their email and starting password and ask them to change it.

Turn bot self-registration on or off

Go to Settings (ማስተካከያዎች) and under Self-registration switch on Let newcomers register themselves. While it is on, anyone whose phone number is not yet on a member record can register themselves through the Telegram bot. Turn it off after a registration drive; the change takes effect within a few seconds.

Everything else

The office can also do everything described for department administrators below (attendance, courses, exams, assets), for every department. The Audit log (ኦዲት) shows who changed what and when.

Role guide

Department administratorየአገልግሎት ክፍል አስተዳዳሪ

A department administrator runs their own department or departments, including every sub-department under them. They see members of their departments but cannot register or edit member records; ask the office for that.

Own departments and sub-departmentsCannot edit member records

Your department's people

  • Open Departments, then your department, to add members to its roster and set their roles.
  • Open any of your members to see their details, attendance, courses and exams.
  • Organise Service groups and manage the Classes your department runs.

Attendance

  1. Go to Attendance (አቴንዳንስ) and press New session.
  2. Choose what it is for: a class level, a service group, or another gathering such as a meeting. The roster fills itself from that choice.
  3. Set the date and start time. People who check in more than 15 minutes after the start are marked Late automatically; you can change the minutes.
  4. Take attendance by scanning QR codes or tapping each person's status. When it is over, press Close session. Press Reopen later if you need to fix a mistake.

Unlike attendance takers, department administrators can create and change sessions for past or future days. Attendance → Reports shows who comes most and who has dropped below a chosen rate, so you know whom to follow up.

Courses

Under Courses (ኮርሶች), register a course, schedule a run (a round of the course with dates), add the students and assign the teachers. Teachers then set up the assessments and enter marks; you can do the same.

Exams

Exams are department tests that are not part of a course, for example the St. John's Wereb assessment of the Mezmur Class. Each exam belongs to a class, optionally to one level of it.

  1. Go to Exams (ፈተናዎች), press New exam, choose the class and, if it is only for one level, that level.
  2. Add the parts and their weights. For example እንቅስቃሴ 40% and ዜማ 60%. The weights must add up to 100. The pass mark is 50 unless you change it.
  3. Add candidates: a whole level or the whole class in one press, or individual members by searching their name or ID. Anyone who joins the class later can be added the same way.
  4. Enter each person's marks on the mark sheet. Each part is marked out of its weight, so ዜማ is out of 60. A total and pass or fail appear once every part is marked.

Assets

Under Assets (ንብረት), record what your department owns, with photos, so it can be found and counted.

Your attendance takers and teachers

In Users, you can create accounts for attendance takers and teachers, only within your own departments. Only the office can create another department administrator.

Role guide

Attendance takerአቴንዳንስ ያዥ

An attendance taker only sees the attendance page, and only today's sessions for their own departments.

Today onlyOwn departments
  1. Sign in and open Attendance. Today's sessions for your departments are listed.
  2. Open the session, or press New session if today's has not been created yet.
  3. Press Start camera and let the camera read each member's QR code, from their printed card or from the bot on their phone. Each scan marks them Present, or Late if they arrive after the late time.
  4. For anyone without a code, find their name on the roster and tap their status: Present (ተገኝቷል), Late (አርፍዷል), Absent (ቀርቷል) or Excused (ፈቃድ).
  5. If the camera cannot read a card, type the member ID into the box under the camera and press Record.
  6. When everyone has been marked, press Close session.
Missed a day?Attendance takers cannot record yesterday's attendance. Ask your department administrator, who can.

Role guide

Teacherመምህር

A teacher sees only the course runs they are assigned to teach.

  1. Sign in and open Courses. Only your own runs are listed.
  2. Open a run and set up its assessments, for example a mid-term and a final, with how much each counts.
  3. Enter each student's marks. The total and pass or fail are worked out for you.

Your students can see their marks in the Telegram bot under 🎓 ኮርሶች. As a member yourself, the same button in the bot also lists the runs you are teaching.

For new members

Registering yourself in the botበቦቱ ራስን መመዝገብ

This works only while the office has turned on self-registration. It takes about five minutes, and the bot asks for everything the office would.

  1. Start the bot and share your phone number. If it is not on any record, the bot shows what it will ask. Press 📝 ልመዝገብ (Register me).
  2. Answer one question at a time. Type your answer or press a button. A bar at the top shows how far along you are.
  3. At the end, the bot shows everything you entered. If it is all correct, press ✅ ይመዝገብ.
  4. You receive your member ID, for example FE-02015. The menu appears and you can use the bot straight away.

Members without Telegram can see the same details in any browser. No password is needed.

Help

Common questions

The bot says my number is not in the records.
Your phone number must be on your member record exactly. Ask the office to check or add it, then share your number in the bot again. If registration is open, the bot instead offers to register you.
The bot says self-registration is closed.
The office has switched it off. Register at the office, or wait for the next registration drive.
Two of us use the same phone.
Register both members with that number. The bot asks whose details to show, and 👥 አባል ቀይር switches between them.
The member page says the ID and phone do not match.
Check the ID and that you typed the number on your record. After several wrong tries it asks you to wait a few minutes before trying again.
A staff page I need is missing from my menu.
Your role does not include it, or you are not assigned to that department. Ask the office to check your account in Users.
An attendance mark is wrong.
Open the session and tap the right status. If the session is closed, reopen it first. Attendance takers can only change today's sessions; for earlier days, ask a department administrator.
A self-registered member's details look wrong.
The office opens the member and edits the record. Use the Registered via → Self-registered (bot) filter to review everyone who registered themselves.
